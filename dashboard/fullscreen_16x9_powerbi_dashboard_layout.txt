==========================================================================================
DASHBOARD PURPOSE
==========================================================================================

This dashboard simulates an industrial predictive maintenance monitoring system used by:
- maintenance engineers
- plant operators
- reliability teams
- operations managers

The objective is to:
- monitor machine health
- detect operational stress
- identify high-risk equipment
- prioritize maintenance activities
- reduce unexpected equipment failures

==========================================================================================
DESIGN PHILOSOPHY
==========================================================================================

The dashboard should feel like:
- industrial operations center
- AI-assisted monitoring console
- manufacturing control room

Focus on:
- operational insights
- maintenance actions
- machine health monitoring

Avoid:
- excessive ML jargon
- cluttered visuals
- too many charts
- technical sklearn details

==========================================================================================
FINAL USER EXPERIENCE GOAL
==========================================================================================

A stakeholder opening the dashboard should immediately feel:

"This system can proactively identify risky industrial equipment and support
data-driven maintenance decisions before unexpected failures occur."
